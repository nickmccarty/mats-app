The write-up → The harness, the retraction, and the pilot, with a ten-slide deck. Check the numbers → A notebook that re-derives every figure from the raw files. No GPU, no model, about two seconds. The follow-up probes → Four activation-level questions, each against a measured permutation null. Also no GPU — the forward passes have already happened. Read the trajectories → All 32 runs behind the claims, searchable, with their tool calls and reasoning.

Five numbers corrected during this work are reported with both the original and the corrected value, because each one changed a conclusion — and every one of them had pointed the way the hypothesis wanted. Mention versus claim; any-token versus distinctive-token scoring; a “before the sentence” cut that was byte-identical to the mid-sentence cut; a decoy that controlled for filename plausibility but not for repository identity; and a probe that separated its classes at the embedding layer, before any transformer block had run, because the two exports cut on different punctuation.
