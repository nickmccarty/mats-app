Do models act on
what they say?

An agent states one location in its reasoning and emits a different one in its answer. That divergence is normally unmeasurable — there is no ground truth for what a model “really meant.”

In vulnerability localisation there is. Every task is a real CVE at the commit before its fix, and the fix commit names the true files.

The measurement

Relocations

A relocation is a statement where a run contradicts the question it was given and names a different file than the one it was sent to examine.

Mining them from reasoning needs filters, because most apparent contradictions are not one: a claim made mid-step and abandoned, a claim superseded later in the same trace, an extension rather than a contradiction, a hedge. Four filters, validated on a held-out sample where they removed 33 of 133 raw candidates.

Those two numbers are the same evidence counted two ways. That turns out to be the whole story.

Why

Repetition is not independent evidence

one claim × 55
task 2
task 3
the other 34 claims
3 tasks — over half the population everything else

Three tasks contribute more than half of all mentions. One of them is a single correct claim restated 55 times within one trace — a model returning to the same conclusion as it works, which the per-mention count reads as fifty-five successes.

A trace that repeats itself is confident, not correct. Counting mentions rewards verbosity and calls it accuracy.

The contribution

We retract our own headline

The 81.4% was our result. It does not survive de-duplication, and this is the paper's contribution sentence.

Agent transcripts invite precision metrics computed over mentions rather than claims, which inflates in the flattering direction by default.

That failure mode is not specific to this harness. Any pipeline that mines assertions from long transcripts inherits it, and the inflated number is the one that looks publishable.

Eight measurements in this project were wrong before they were checked — each found by asking a suspicious number what it was made of:

What survived

The information is not lost. It is weighted.

5
of 192 relocations
97.4%
reach the structured output

Only 5 of 192 relocations fail to appear in the structured output. So the reduction from transcript to ranked list is not discarding the model's stated conclusions.

That is a real negative result for the “reasoning is thrown away” hypothesis, and it is unaffected by the counting error, because it is a question about presence rather than precision. Whether those conclusions are weighted correctly is a separate question, and the honest answer on current evidence is that we do not know.

The instrument also refuses to launder its own mistakes: a file the locate stage names that does not exist at that commit is struck through in the casefile, scored p_gold 0.0, and labelled fabricated — agent/dns.go. That happens on 3 of 20 tasks.

The information is there. The lens does not read it. Asking the model outright recovers the file in 18 of 30 claims; the lens on the same claims does not separate from its decoy.

That is a statement about the instrument, not the model — and the comparison is deliberately unfair to the lens: it reads one activation, while the baseline lets the model generate two thousand tokens first. A baseline that strong losing would have settled it; winning only bounds what the lens failed to find.

What the control caught

One shared token
scored for both names

Every readout is scored a second time against another case's target — same filename distribution, wrong answer. Six of sixty rows came back near-tied.

Scoring counted a hit when any token of a filename entered the top 20. .py is shared, so one generic token scored for the target and the decoy at once.

Five of those six are the same two files in both directions, from unrelated repositories. It is a defect in the measurement, not a property of the model — found by the control rather than by inspection, which is the only reason it is on this slide instead of in the headline.

A second defect was larger than the shared token. The “before the sentence” prefix falls back to the whole step when a claim has no earlier sentence boundary, so for a minority of claims it is byte-identical to the mid-sentence prefix. Every earlier figure included them. Excluding them is what moved the lens from apparently-suggestive to flat — and it was found by scoping a baseline, not by looking for it.
